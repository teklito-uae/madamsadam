# 11 — Branding section

Depends on: 02-motion-primitives

## Objective
Design-magazine-style section showcasing brand identity/campaign/digital work. Copy in [../../CONTENT_GUIDE.md](../../CONTENT_GUIDE.md#branding-section).

## Steps
1. Heading `MAKE YOUR BRAND UNFORGETTABLE.` via `RevealText`.
2. Composition of floating posters/editorial layout elements, brand identity boards, website screens, photography — layered/overlapping, not a grid of equal boxes.
3. Explicitly avoid laptop/device mockup clichés — use flat screen crops, floating cards at angles, or framed compositions instead.
4. Use `RevealImage` for each visual element, staggered entrance.

## Acceptance criteria
- No literal laptop/phone mockup frames.
- Composition reads as an art-directed layout, not a repeated card pattern.
