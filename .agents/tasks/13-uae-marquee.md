# 13 — UAE identity + marquee

Depends on: 02-motion-primitives

## Objective
Communicate UAE/local identity editorially, plus a continuous marquee band. Copy in [../../CONTENT_GUIDE.md](../../CONTENT_GUIDE.md#uae-identity) and [../../CONTENT_GUIDE.md](../../CONTENT_GUIDE.md#marquee).

## Steps
1. `BASED IN UAE. BUILT FOR THE WORLD.` section — imagery of nightlife, architecture, people, culture (not skyline stock photos).
2. Marquee band(s) using the `Marquee` primitive: `CREATE ✦ CONNECT ✦ BECOME` and/or `EVENTS — STORIES — PEOPLE — BRANDS — CULTURE — EXPERIENCES`. Alternate scroll direction if more than one marquee is used on the page.

## Acceptance criteria
- Marquee loops seamlessly with no visible jump/reset.
- Marquee pauses under `prefers-reduced-motion` or reduces to a static line.
